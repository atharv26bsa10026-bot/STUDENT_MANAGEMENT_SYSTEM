 1/-Problem Statement:
Before the school/colleges have to fill the details of student manually and it may have some errors also. but now The Student Management System calculates and save a simple solution to store student information, manage subject-wise marks, calculate total marks and percentages, and assign grades.

2/-Scope of the Project
-Storing student names and registration numbers.
-Recording marks for SUBJECT 1,2,3
-Calculating total marks, percentages, and  assign the grades.
-Displaying all student records.
-Searching for students using registration numbers.

3/-Target Users
-Students:To understand and manage basic records.
-Teachers: To view student marks and Details.
-Educational Institutions:To demonstrate a simple student record management system.
-Beginners: To learn Python programming through a practical project.

4/-High-Level Features
1.Add Student: Enter student name, registration number, and subject-wise marks.
2.Display Records: View all saved student details.
3.Search Student: Find a student using their registration number.
4.Calculate Total: Automatically calculate the total marks of three subjects.
5.Calculate Percentage: Calculate the average percentage based on the marks entered.
6.Grade Assignment: Assign grades according to the student's percentage.
